# Models

YOLO weights live here. None are in the repository: the stock ones (`yolo26s-seg.pt` and the rest) download
here the first time they're picked in the Camera tab, and `slixer/train.py` copies models you train here too.
Any `.pt` file put here shows up in the Camera tab's list. See [VISION.md](../VISION.md).

The stock weights are Ultralytics', under the AGPL-3.0 (or an Ultralytics Enterprise licence).

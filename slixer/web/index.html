      How to line the model up with your arm
      1. Be in Watch, with the arm reporting (the pill at the top left says what it is doing).
      2. Move one joint by hand at a time. Its arm reading changes, and the model should turn the same way.
      3. If it turns the wrong way, press flip for that joint.
      4. If it turns the right way but sits at an angle, type an offset until the model matches.
      5. Or put the real arm where the model's zero pose is (Joints → zero pose, in Plan, shows it) and press this pose is zero to set every offset at once. Get the signs right first.
      6. Press save. It's kept in slixer/mapping.json.

      Recorded programs replay correctly even before this is done — they store the arm's own angles and turn them back the same way. Lining up matters for the picture, for dragging the gripper, and for sending it to a part.
